Questions? Answered

What is a link in bio?

It is one link in your social profile that opens a page with all your other links, so followers can find everything you share in one place.

What makes Winktree better than the other link in bio options?

Setup takes minutes, the page is easy to customize, and built-in analytics show which links your audience actually opens.

How can I drive more traffic to and through my Winktree?

Put your link in every bio, add it to posts and videos, share it as a QR code offline, and keep your top link fresh.

How many links should I have on my Winktree?

Start with the few that matter most. Fewer, clearer links usually get more clicks than a long list.

Do I need a website to use Winktree?

No. Your page can be your home on the web, and it can point to any site, store or profile you already have.
